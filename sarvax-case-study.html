The Challenge

Hiring was running the team — not the other way around.

The company's 3-person HR team was managing 18 open roles across product, engineering, and operations. Every candidate required the same manual sequence: CV review in email, shortlisting via spreadsheet, WhatsApp scheduling back-and-forth, an initial screening call that ran 45–60 minutes, manual note-taking, and a written summary forwarded to the hiring manager.

At 18 active roles with an average of 12 candidates per role, the team was running 200+ hours of pure screening and coordination work per quarter — before a single hire was made. Roles that should have closed in 3 weeks were dragging to 8.

The bottleneck was not the talent market. It was the process. Every step waited for a coordinator to push it forward. The team had no capacity to handle more roles, no consistency across different interviewers, and no structured scoring system to compare candidates objectively.

"We were spending more time scheduling calls than actually evaluating people. The process owned us — we didn't own the process."

The Solution

SARVAX HR Interview Bot: The process runs itself.

  • AI generates tailored interview questions from the JD and candidate resume — no manual prep
  • Bot joins the Google Meet session and conducts the interview autonomously
  • Instant fit score generated post-interview with strengths, red flags, and structured rationale
  • Full report automatically emailed to the HR manager — no coordinator involvement
  • Complete candidate pipeline tracked in live dashboard with fit percentages and status

SARVAX deployed its HR Interview Bot into the company's recruitment pipeline. The integration was straightforward: HR uploads the job description and candidate CVs. The agent takes it from there.

Questions are generated from the intersection of the JD and each candidate's specific resume — meaning every interview is tailored, not templated. The bot joins the scheduled Google Meet link, conducts the full screening session, and produces a structured evaluation with fit score, candidate strengths, and flagged concerns.

The HR manager receives a complete report in their inbox — without scheduling a debrief, without reading through notes, without chasing a coordinator for a summary. The pipeline dashboard gives every stakeholder real-time visibility on candidate status and scoring across all active roles.

The team's coordinators were removed from the screening execution layer entirely. They now handle offer negotiation, candidate relationship management, and strategic workforce planning — the work they were hired for.
