01 / The problem

The team keeps its meeting, document, message, and tracking tools. KARAX reduces the work of copying the same information between them.

Before KARAX / industry-informed baseline

The call ended. The admin began.

A consultant or coordinator had to turn a live conversation into something the rest of the firm could use.

  • Attend the meeting and take rough notes
  • Rewrite and format the notes after the call
  • Extract decisions, owners, and dates manually
  • Send follow-up through WhatsApp or email
  • Copy important items into Calendar, Sheets, or CRM
  • Search old threads when context was needed again
With KARAX / focused capability

The next steps were ready.

KARAX prepared the record. A person checked the result and approved anything that left the platform.

  • Open the meeting record
  • Review the notes, owners, dates, and details
  • Approve the follow-up or task
  • Send the message or update the connected system
  • Keep the approved record with the client context
  • Find what was agreed when it is needed again

Works with the tools already in place.

05 / Impact

The value is not removing the consultant. It is giving the consultant less meeting administration and more time to act on the work agreed.

About 5h 28m returned every week.
