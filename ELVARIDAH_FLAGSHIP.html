The business question

What happens after the meeting ends?

Without a clear handover, the team has to rebuild the conversation from rough notes, messages, and memory. KARAX prepares the handover so the team can review the facts and act on what was agreed.

BEFORENotes, actions, and follow-up are rebuilt manually.
WITH KARAXThe meeting becomes one prepared working record.
CONTROLA person approves the result before it moves.

05 / Existing tools

KARAX is useful when it fits the team’s working environment. The point is not to replace every tool. The point is to carry approved meeting information into the right one.

Integration principle

Use the tools the team already knows.

Meeting capture, documents, messages, calendars, and trackers can continue doing their jobs. KARAX connects the handover between them, subject to permission and client approval.

08 / Validation

The next step is not a broad rollout. It is a controlled comparison using the same meeting type, the same team, and the same approved tools.

Proposed validation

Measure one week of real meeting follow-up.

Use 5–10 comparable client meetings. Record the current time spent on notes, actions, follow-up, and tracker updates. Then compare it with KARAX preparation plus human review.

Replace the illustrative example with approved meeting records, verified timings, and a client-approved outcome before calling this a results case study.

Success checks

What the client should be able to verify

  1. Time from meeting end to usable notes
  2. Time from meeting end to approved follow-up
  3. Actions captured with the right owners and dates
  4. Actions still open after seven days
  5. Human review time and edits required

Where client feedback plugs in:

Week 1 · TrustThe team compares KARAX notes with its own. Accuracy of decisions, owners, and dates decides whether the pilot continues.
Week 2 · FeelShort interviews: what felt safe, what felt slow, what still needs a human hand. Concerns are fixed before measurement hardens.
Weeks 3–4 · NumbersThe success checks above are measured on real meetings. The illustrative 5h 28m model is replaced by client data.
Week 4 · DecisionGo or no-go. Approved quotes, screenshots, and metrics become the evidence layer of this page.
